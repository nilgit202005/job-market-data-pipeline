Mapping the data analyst job market

A solo-built pipeline that pulls live job postings from a public API, cleans and models them, and turns them into a dashboard of in-demand skills and real salary ranges — across India, the UK, and the US.

300+ postings analyzed · 3 countries · 24 skills tracked

How it works

Five stages, each one doing a specific job — extract, store, clean, and visualize.

Adzuna API→ Python→ Postgres (Docker)→ dbt→ Power BI

Postings are fetched by country and role, loaded raw into Postgres, then modeled with dbt into two clean tables — one for skill mentions, one for salary by role. Power BI reads directly from the warehouse.

Caught along the way: an early skills query matched the letter “R” inside words like “for” and “your”, inflating its count 50×. Fixed with word-boundary matching. A second pass caught salaries from three currencies being compared without conversion — normalized everything to USD before it reached the dashboard.
